Background

Benny was born in New York City, grew up in Baltimore, Maryland, then returned to New York, where he played drums and percussion for songwriters, bands, and dozens of musicals, accompanied modern-dance classes, and taught drum students of all ages. In 2014, following two years of touring internationally with Swear and Shake, Benny moved to Nashville, Tennessee. He currently lives in Northern California.
