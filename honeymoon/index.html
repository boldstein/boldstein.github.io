Benny & Ari's Japan Honeymoon

JAL nonstop SFO ⇆ HND · Arrive Nov 15, 5:10 PM

The post-Copper honeymoon we've been saving up points for. Thirteen nights, just the two of us. We start in Tokyo Metropolis, slow down in the old Japanese cities of Kanazawa and Kyoto, soak in a private onsen at a historic ryokan outside Osaka, spend a night at a Buddhist mountain temple, and finish with two days of Disney magic. We're flying Premium Economy both ways, which already feels like the honeymoon starting early.

Phase 1 · The Apéritif

Tokyo

4 NIGHTS
4 Nights
Out exploring Tokyo

Four nights based in Shinjuku, which sits right on the Yamanote Line loop, providing easy access to both sides of the city. On the West side, we plan to explore vintage shops, good coffee, and perhaps catch a glimpse of Harajuku and Shibuya. On the East side, we'll take in an older, quieter Tokyo, including ancient temples, artisan craft workshops, izakayas filled with salarymen, arcades of Akihabara, and the Tokyo Skytree.

Coffee & Food
  • Glitch Coffee & RoastersCoffee Shop
    A well-known third-wave coffee roaster in Kanda, popular with serious coffee people.
  • Bear Pond EspressoCoffee Shop
    A well-known Shimokitazawa espresso bar, run by one intense, exacting barista. Better than the older, smokier kissaten cafes nearby.
  • Meikyoku Kissa LionJazz Kissaten
    A listening bar in Shibuya running since 1926. You sit in silence and listen to classical and jazz records on a huge vintage sound system, no talking allowed.
  • Shinbashi YokochoFood Alley
    A narrow alley of small bars and food stalls, tucked under the train tracks. This is the classic after-work spot where Tokyo's salarymen go to drink.
Landmarks
  • Tokyo Metro Government BuildingObservation Deck
    Free observation deck, 202 meters up, with Mount Fuji visible on a clear day.
  • Senso-ji & AsakusaOld Tokyo
    Tokyo's oldest temple, with the Nakamise shopping street leading up to it. The old-downtown counterpart to the west side's modern sprawl.
Neighborhoods
  • ShimokitazawaNeighborhood
    A neighborhood known for vintage clothing shops, live music venues, and narrow, winding streets.
  • KoenjiNeighborhood
    A scrappier, less-touristed neighborhood known for tiny record shops, punk bars, and hole-in-the-wall live-music venues.
Phase 2 · Main Course

Old Japan, By Rail

Tokyo to Kanazawa, then Kanazawa to Kyoto. Two old, beautiful cities. Kanazawa's a feudal-era castle town on the Sea of Japan that most visitors skip entirely. Kyoto was Japan's imperial capital for over a thousand years, filled with temples, gardens, palaces, and culture.

Kanazawa 2 Nights

A feudal-era castle town by the sea, with one of Japan's great gardens and an intact samurai quarter, without the crowds Kyoto gets.

Kyoto 2 Nights

Japan's ancient imperial capital, over a thousand years of temples, gardens, and geisha districts. We picked our favorites instead of rushing the whole city.

This is the leg where the pace changes. We trade Kyoto's crowds for a train down to Namba in Osaka, then switch onto the Nankai Koya Line out to Kawachinagano, a small town most tourists never see since it's really just a stop on the way up to Koyasan. Check-in at Nanten-en opens at 3 PM and closes at 6, so we're leaving Kyoto by early afternoon, no reason to rush into our first private onsen soak.

Phase 3 · Palate Cleanser

Nanten-en

2 NIGHTS
2 Nights · Seiryuu-tei

A 1913 ryokan in Kawachinagano, designed by Kingo Tatsuno, the same architect behind Tokyo Station. We booked the Seiryuu-tei room, the only suite with its own open-air private onsen. Two nights, nowhere to be. Vegan breakfast is guaranteed, and the concierge has already lined up a few nearby vegan spots for us too, just in case we want to leave the ryokan at all.

  • RyokanTraditional Inn
    A traditional Japanese hot spring inn, combining classic lodging and multi-course meals with access to mineral-rich geothermal bath waters for relaxation and cultural immersion.
  • Tatami RoomsRoom Style
    Sliding paper doors (fusuma), straw tatami floors, and low tables, with staff rolling out padded futon beds at night.
  • YukataGuest Attire
    Comfortable cotton robes worn throughout the inn and the surrounding hot spring town.

Koyasan

1 NIGHT
1 Night · Temple Stay

A quiet mountain reset after two nights of onsen soaking. The food is vegan by tradition, mornings include a meditation session, and we're doing a night tour through a 1,200-year-old cemetery.

  • OkunoinCemetery, Night Tour
    Japan's largest cemetery, a forested path lined with over 200,000 lanterns and old stone markers. Especially atmospheric at night.
